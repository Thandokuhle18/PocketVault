# PocketVault: Business Brief

**Tagline:** Home for your finances.
**One-line pitch:** PocketVault helps small business owners to plan, track and protect their money so that they stay profitable and debt-free.

## 1. The problem
Millions of South Africans run or want to start small businesses (home bakeries, spaza shops, car washes, salons, freelancers). Most manage money in a notebook, a spreadsheet or WhatsApp messages. Three things go wrong:

1. **They start without knowing the numbers.** A home baker with R3,500 rent, R1,800 electricity and R2,200 stock must earn at least R7,500 a month before making a cent of profit. Most never calculate this.
2. **They slide into debt without warning.** Bills (rent, loan repayments, electricity) fall due while the cash has already been spent. The owner only finds out when the money is gone.
3. **Money leaks and nobody can prove where.** When a business has staff, cash purchases and sales are easy to alter, split or hide. Owners have no record of who captured or changed what.

**Who has it:** pre-launch entrepreneurs, informal and micro-business owners, freelancers, small retail and service businesses.
**How often:** every month, at bill time, and every time cash changes hands.
**What it costs today:** late fees, loans taken to cover shortfalls, stock bought with money needed for rent, undetected theft, and businesses that close because the idea never worked on paper.

## 2. The customer
- **User (Owner):** a small business owner or aspiring owner, 20–55, on a smartphone or laptop, with little or no accounting knowledge.
- **User (Staff):** a part-time or full-time employee who buys stock or takes payments.
- **Who pays:** the owner, by monthly subscription.

## 3. The solution (one sentence)
PocketVault is a plain-language web app where a business owner plans the numbers before spending, records every rand in and out, sees a Debt Guard warning before bills become debt, and keeps staff handling money accountable through approvals, an audit trail and integrity flags.

## 4. Must features on day one
1. Account, business setup and staff invites (Owner and Staff roles)
2. Business Financial Planner (startup costs, break-even, funding gap)
3. Income and Expense Ledger (with audit trail)
4. Dashboard with Debt Guard (safe-to-spend and risk level)
5. Bills (upcoming, overdue, mark paid)

Should: staff approvals, and integrity flags (large cash, split payments, duplicates, edits, deletions).

## 5. Impact and how we measure it
| What changes for the owner | How we measure it |
|---|---|
| Knows if the idea is financially realistic before spending | % of new owners who complete a plan with 3+ lines in 7 days |
| Sees shortfalls before they become debt | % of owner-days with status "Safe" vs "At risk"; bills paid before the due date |
| Keeps a reliable daily record | % of owners with 10+ transactions in 14 days |
| Staff-handled money is accountable | % of flags marked Reviewed; transactions with a full audit trail (target 100%) |

## 6. Business model
Subscription in rand. Proposed starting prices, to be tested with real owners:
| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | R0 | 1 business, planner, up to 50 transactions a month, Debt Guard |
| Starter | R79 a month | Unlimited transactions, bills, 2 staff |
| Business | R199 a month | Up to 5 staff, approvals, integrity flags, audit log |

All features are unlocked in the capstone MVP. Pricing is a hypothesis, not a validated number.

## 7. Go-to-market: first 100 customers
1. **Founder-led, first 20:** walk into 20 small businesses near me (bakers, barbers, car washes) and set up their plan with them in 15 minutes.
2. **Communities, next 30:** WhatsApp and Facebook groups for township entrepreneurs, stokvels, and student entrepreneurship societies. A free "Will my business make a profit?" planner link is the hook.
3. **Short videos, next 30:** TikTok and YouTube Shorts showing a real plan built in 2 minutes.
4. **Partners, next 20:** small-business support bodies (e.g. SEDA offices), bookkeepers and accountants who refer clients that are too small for full accounting software.

## 8. Competition and why PocketVault
| Alternative | Gap |
|---|---|
| Notebook and spreadsheets | No warnings, no staff control, easy to alter |
| Full accounting software (Xero, Sage, Zoho Books, QuickBooks) | Built for bookkeepers, accounting jargon, no pre-launch planning, priced for established firms |
| Bank business apps | Only see money inside that bank account |

PocketVault combines **plan before you start and track after you start** in one tool, in plain language, in rand, mobile-first, with Debt Guard and staff controls.

## 9. What PocketVault does not claim
- It does not guarantee a business will be profitable or debt-free. It shows the owner's own numbers and warns early.
- Integrity flags are advisory. They are not accusations, and PocketVault does not report to any authority or replace legal compliance.
- It is not accounting, tax or payroll software.
